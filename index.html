Right-size this for your program

This works whether you have eight children or two hundred, whether you run a home-based program or a multi-site organization, and whether you have a full leadership team or it is mostly you.

One question first. Who is the person closest to running things when you are not there?

Look at SHARED first

SHARED almost always means one of two things. Either they cannot hold it yet, or you have not let go of it.

Be honest about which one it is, line by line. Those are very different problems with very different fixes, and most owners assume it is the first when it is the second.

Exercise 2

Five questions about authority

Answer these about your Director. If nobody holds that seat yet, answer them about the person you would most likely grow into it.

1
When they make a decision, do they need your approval?
2
Do they understand the numbers their work affects?
3
Do they know what should come to you and what should not?
4
Do you let them make decisions differently than you would?
5
Do you take responsibilities back when something goes wrong?

Someone with responsibilities but no authority is still an assistant to the owner, whatever their title says.

Exercise 3

Your 30-day action

Choose three things you currently hold that could appropriately move to someone else this month. Small counts. “Ordering supplies” counts. Write the thing, the boundary, and the date you say it out loud.

  1. Transfer one
    What they decide, and what still comes to you
  2. Transfer two
  3. Transfer three
Write it down. It is usually smaller on paper than it is in your head.
The part nobody warns you about

They will do at least one of these differently than you would have. That is not a sign it failed.

Support sounds like: what have you tried, what are you thinking, what do you need from me. Rescue sounds like: give it to me.
